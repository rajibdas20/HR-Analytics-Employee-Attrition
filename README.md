# HR Analytics – Employee Attrition

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes employee data to understand **employee attrition, workforce trends, and key HR metrics**. The analysis helps identify factors associated with employee turnover and provides insights that can support data-driven HR decisions.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall employee attrition
* Identify departments with higher attrition
* Analyze employee demographics and job-related factors
* Understand patterns in employee turnover
* Track key HR performance indicators
* Build an interactive dashboard for HR analysis

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Power BI** – Dashboard development and data visualization
* **Excel / CSV** – Data preparation and analysis
* **Data Analytics** – KPI analysis and business insights

## 📈 Key Analysis Areas

* Employee Attrition Rate
* Total Employees
* Active Employees
* Attrition by Department
* Attrition by Job Role
* Attrition by Age Group
* Attrition by Gender
* Attrition by Job Satisfaction
* Attrition by Years at Company

## 📊 Dashboard

The project includes an interactive HR Analytics dashboard designed to provide a clear overview of employee attrition and workforce-related KPIs.

## 💡 Key Insights

The analysis focuses on identifying employee groups and organizational factors associated with higher attrition, helping HR teams better understand workforce turnover and potential retention areas.
